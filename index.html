Featured add-on

Mat-Baker

Bake procedural materials into PBR texture packs in one click. Pick an engine preset and a resolution, then export. Presets for Unreal, Unity, glTF and Godot.

  • Base Color, Normal, Roughness, Metallic, Height, AO, Emission, Opacity and Curvature maps
  • ORM and Mask-map channel packing
  • Models with many materials baked into one texture set
  • Bakes a copy, so your original material is never touched

LIPI ලිපි

A small Windows program that turns what you type in English letters (Singlish) into Sinhala letters the moment you type it.

  • Type Singlish in any app and get Sinhala
  • Pick Unicode or Legacy (FM Abhaya) output
  • Right Ctrl turns Sinhala typing on or off, Right Shift switches the output
