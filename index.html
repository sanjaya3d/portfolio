Mat-Baker

Bake procedural materials into PBR texture packs in one click. Pick an engine preset and a resolution, then export. Presets for Unreal, Unity, glTF and Godot.

  • ORM and Mask-map channel packing
  • Models with many materials baked into one texture set
  • Bakes a copy, so your original material is never touched
